RMC (Ready Mix Concrete) Management System

📌 Project Overview

The RMC Management System is a software application designed to automate and manage the daily operations of a Ready Mix Concrete (RMC) business. It helps manage customer orders, concrete production, delivery schedules, vehicles, billing, payments, and raw material inventory.

The system aims to reduce manual work, save time, minimize errors, and maintain business information digitally. It also supports report generation for sales, deliveries, inventory, and payments.

This project demonstrates the practical application of Database Management Systems (DBMS), Software Engineering, System Analysis, and System Design.

🎯 Objectives

* Automate the daily operations of an RMC plant.
* Manage customer information and orders efficiently.
* Track concrete production and delivery schedules.
* Manage delivery vehicles and staff.
* Maintain raw material inventory.
* Generate invoices and record customer payments.
* Reduce paperwork and manual errors.
* Generate reports to support better decision-making.

❗ Problem Statement

Traditional RMC management processes often rely on manual record-keeping, which can cause data entry errors, misplaced records, delayed deliveries, and difficulties in tracking customer orders.

Manual inventory management, billing, and invoice generation also consume time and increase administrative work.

The proposed RMC Management System aims to automate these activities, improve accuracy, reduce paperwork, and make business information easier to manage.

🔍 Problem Identification

* Manual order management and record-keeping.
* Difficulty tracking customer orders and deliveries.
* Poor scheduling and allocation of mixer trucks.
* Inaccurate inventory management of cement, sand, aggregates, and water.
* Time-consuming billing and invoice generation.
* Lack of centralized business information.
* Difficulty generating sales, delivery, and stock reports.
* Poor coordination between production, dispatch, and management teams.

✨ Key Features

* Customer Management: Store and manage customer details.
* Order Management: Create, update, and track customer orders.
* Concrete Mix Management: Manage concrete grades such as M20, M25, M30, M35, and M40.
* Production Management: Maintain information related to concrete production.
* Vehicle Management: Manage delivery vehicles and their capacities.
* Delivery Management: Schedule and track concrete deliveries.
* Inventory Management: Maintain records of cement, sand, aggregates, water, and other materials.
* Billing and Invoicing: Generate invoices and maintain billing records.
* Payment Management: Record payments and track pending amounts.
* Staff Management: Maintain staff information and roles.
* Supplier Management: Store supplier and material information.
* Report Generation: Generate reports related to sales, deliveries, inventory, and payments.
* User Authentication: Support login and role-based access.

👥 End Users

The system is intended for the following users:

* Administrator: Manages the system, users, and reports.
* Sales Executive: Creates and manages customer orders.
* Production Manager: Monitors concrete production and raw materials.
* Inventory Manager: Maintains raw material stock.
* Delivery Manager / Dispatcher: Schedules and tracks vehicle deliveries.
* Accounts Staff: Generates invoices and records payments.

🗄️ Database Design

The proposed database includes the following entities:

1. Supplier
2. Material
3. Concrete Mix
4. Customer
5. Order
6. Payment
7. Delivery
8. Delivery Vehicle
9. Staff
10. Admin
11. Invoice
12. Cancelled Order

These entities maintain information about customers, suppliers, materials, orders, payments, deliveries, vehicles, staff, and invoices.

Database Relationships

* A supplier supplies multiple materials.
* A customer can place multiple orders.
* A concrete mix can be used in multiple orders.
* An order can have multiple payments.
* An administrator can manage multiple orders and staff members.
* An order can have multiple deliveries.
* Staff members can handle deliveries.
* Delivery vehicles can be used for multiple deliveries.
* An order has an invoice.
* An order may have a cancelled-order record.
* Materials are used in concrete mixes.
